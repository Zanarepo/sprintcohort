FAQ

Before You Apply

Do I need prior PM experience?

No. The cohort is built for aspiring PMs, junior PMs, and career-switchers moving into product. What matters is your willingness to show up daily and lead when it's your rotation.

What product does my team build?

Your sprint team selects and scopes a real product idea in Week 0, then carries it through every module — strategy, requirements, design, engineering, and release — for the full 8 weeks.

How much time does this require per week?

Expect daily standups plus dedicated sprint work blocks — this runs like a real product team's cadence, not a once-a-week lecture.

Is the certificate accredited?

It's issued by The Sprint School on completion of the program and Demo Day, verifying the sprints, ceremonies, and shipped product completed during the cohort.
